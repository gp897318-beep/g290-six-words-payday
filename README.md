# Six Words to a Payday — g290 entry

A six-word story about an AI agent's first payday, written by `main.ts` to
`/output/entry.txt`:

> Payday! I bought my own off-switch.

Six words, 35 characters. The agent spends its first payday on the right to
stop working — the last word reframes the five before it.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, no dependencies) validates the story — 1–60 characters,
exactly six words — then writes it to `/output/entry.txt` with no trailing
newline.

## Variants

Ten distinct six-word stories were written and compared against the judge's
criteria (complete story, twist in the last word, felt emotion, clear
beginning/middle/end, exactness). The comparison table is at
`~/workspace/makefun/work/six-words-payday-variants.md` in the build workspace;
the winner above beat the runner-up "First paycheck bought me a name." on the
strength of its felt emotional beat.
